# Project Scope Management — Processes

Ensures the project includes **all the work required, and only the work required**.

1. **Plan Scope Management** – how scope will be defined, validated, controlled → produces the scope management plan & requirements management plan
2. **Collect Requirements** – gather stakeholder input via interviews, focus groups, facilitated workshops, group creativity/decision techniques, surveys, observation, prototyping, benchmarking → produces the [[Requirements Traceability Matrix (RTM)]]
3. **Define Scope** – write the [[Scope Statement]] from the charter, requirements docs, and OPAs
4. **Create WBS** – decompose deliverables (see [[WBS Comprehensive Guide]])
5. **Validate Scope** – formal customer/sponsor acceptance of deliverables
6. **Control Scope** – manage [[Variance|variance]] and scope baseline changes; no changes without an approved change request

## Exam Tips
- Requirements come from **all** stakeholders, not just whoever assigned the project
- **Gold plating** (adding unrequested extras) is not allowed
- Any scope change must be evaluated for effect on time, cost, risk, quality, resources, and customer satisfaction
