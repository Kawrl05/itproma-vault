# 9. Project Procurement Management

Managing **outside procurement** (e.g., subcontractors) — affects budget & schedule.

- Plan procurement → conduct procurement (SOW, RFP, vendor selection) → control (manage/monitor) → close contracts

> PMP exam tip: assume you're the PM for the **buyer** in all procurement questions.
