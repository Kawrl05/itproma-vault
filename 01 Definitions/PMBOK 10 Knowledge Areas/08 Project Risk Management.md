# 8. Project Risk Management

- Identify risks → build a **risk register**
- Qualitative analysis (likelihood & impact) → prioritize
- Quantitative analysis (impact on budget/schedule)
- Plan risk responses (assign an owner to each risk)
- Control risk – regularly review/retire risks from the register
