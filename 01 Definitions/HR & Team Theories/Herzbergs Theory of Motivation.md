# Herzberg's Theory of Motivation

- **Motivating agents** – relate to the work itself (job satisfaction, responsibility)
- **Hygiene factors** – relate to the work *environment* (policies, working conditions, salary, security)

Hygiene factors are **necessary but not sufficient**: absent → morale/productivity drop; present → morale stays the same (doesn't boost it).
