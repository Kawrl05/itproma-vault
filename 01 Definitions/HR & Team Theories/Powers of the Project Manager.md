# Powers of the Project Manager

1. **Formal (legitimate)** – based on position
2. **Reward** – ability to give rewards
3. **Penalty (coercive)** – ability to penalize
4. **Expert** – based on technical/PM expertise
5. **Referent** – based on people liking/respecting/wanting to be like the PM
