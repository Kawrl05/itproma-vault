# Conflict Management Styles

1. **Confronting/Problem-Solving** – solve the real problem → win-win
2. **Compromising** – both sides give something up → low-lose (nobody gets everything)
3. **Withdrawal/Avoidance** – postpone/retreat from the decision
4. **Smoothing/Accommodating** – emphasize agreement over differences
5. **Collaborating** – incorporate multiple viewpoints → consensus
6. **Forcing** – push one viewpoint at the other's expense

**Why it matters:** improves communication, strengthens teamwork, increases productivity, builds trust, reduces stress.
