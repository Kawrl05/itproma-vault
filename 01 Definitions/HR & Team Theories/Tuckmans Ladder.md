# Tuckman's Ladder (Stages of Team Development)

| Stage | What Happens |
|---|---|
| Forming | Team introduced, roles assigned |
| Storming | Conflicts as people adjust/express opinions |
| Norming | Team builds trust, sets common goals |
| Performing | Team works smoothly & productively |
| Adjourning | Project ends, team disbands |
