# Maslow's Hierarchy of Needs

Motivation comes from an unsatisfied need; **lower needs must be met first**.

1. Physiological – air, water, food, sleep
2. Safety – protection from danger
3. Social – belonging, acceptance, friendship
4. Esteem – recognition, status, appreciation
5. Self-actualization – realizing one's full potential
