# Gantt Chart

A **visual project timeline** showing tasks and their dependencies. Invented by Henry Gantt (1910), inspired by Karol Adamiecki's 1896 harmonogram — one of the oldest and most important PM tools.

Shows what the [[Work Breakdown Structure (Definition)|WBS]] does not: task **dependencies** and duration on a timeline.
