# IT Project Management (ITPM)

The planning, scheduling, execution, monitoring, and reporting of [[IT Project|IT projects]].

IT is unique because nearly every industry has some IT component, so ITPM overlaps with almost every field.

**IT PMs must deal with:**
- Risk
- Interdependent integrations
- Software updates
- Scope creep

## Unique Challenges of IT Project Management
- **Changing Technology** – tech becomes obsolete quickly; ITPM must plan for constant change
- **Communication** – distributed/remote teams across time zones
- **Transparency** – clear deadlines help teams adapt to change quickly
- **Lack of Agreement on Methodology** – without a defined process, projects go off-track and over budget
