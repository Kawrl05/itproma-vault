# Stakeholder

A person or entity, inside or outside the project, with a **key interest** in the project — can influence or be influenced by it (e.g., sponsor, customer, financial controller, CEO).

**Project Stakeholder Management** involves:
1. Identify stakeholders (stakeholder analysis)
2. Plan stakeholder management (list & prioritize concerns)
3. Manage stakeholder engagement (meet expectations)
4. Control stakeholder engagement (adjust as needed)
