# Worked Example: Construction Project SMART Objectives

*(Expanded from the Luxury Apartments at York charter — see [[Example - Construction Charter]])*

1. **Complete** construction of the luxury apartment complex (3 floor plans, 800–1,350 sq ft units) (S), **within the approved $15 million budget** (M/A), **to meet local demand for luxury housing** (R), **within 12–16 months of project start (Q4 start date)** (T).
2. **Maintain** zero budget overrun beyond the built-in contingency fund (S/M), **through active cost tracking by the Construction PM** (A), **to protect the firm's ROI on the project** (R), **through project closeout** (T).
3. **Deliver** the project **within 18 months** at the absolute latest, adding subcontractors if needed without exceeding budget (S/M/A), **to avoid contractual and financial penalties** (R), **as measured against the summary milestone schedule** (T).
