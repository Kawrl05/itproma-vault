# Worked Example: E-Commerce Site SMART Objectives

*(Expanded from the e-commerce scope statement — see [[Scope Statement Examples]])*

1. **Launch** a fully functional, responsive e-commerce website with search, cart, secure checkout, and admin dashboard (S), **supporting up to 1,000 catalog items** (M), **using a team of developers, designers, and a cybersecurity specialist** (A), **to expand market reach and increase online revenue** (R), **within 6 months of project approval** (T).
2. **Keep** total project spend **at or under ₱800,000** (S/M), covering development, hosting, and licensing (A), **to protect the client's approved budget** (R), **through project close (6-month mark)** (T).
3. **Achieve** SSL-encrypted, data-privacy-compliant checkout and account handling (S), **passing 100% of planned security and performance tests** (M), **using the assigned QA/testing plan** (A), **to meet Philippine data privacy and e-commerce law** (R), **prior to launch** (T).
4. **Provide** 90 days of post-launch technical support and admin staff training (S/M), **via the delivery team** (A), **to ensure smooth client handover** (R), **immediately following go-live** (T).
