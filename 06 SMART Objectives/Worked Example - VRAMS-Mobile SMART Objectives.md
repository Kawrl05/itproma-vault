# Worked Example: VRAMS-Mobile SMART Objectives

*(Expanded from the charter's business objectives — see [[Example - VRAMS-Mobile Charter]])*

1. **Reduce** the company's annual appointment servicing cost **from 9% to 5%** of annual operating cost (S/M), **by deploying the VRAMS-Mobile app** (A/R), **within the first year of VRAMS-Mobile operations** (T).
2. **Generate** at least **$400,000 in additional annual revenue** through in-app mobile advertisements (S/M), **by integrating an ad-serving module tied to customer/vehicle profile** (A/R), **within the first 12 months post-launch** (T).
3. **Raise** the customer satisfaction rating for R&M appointments, preventive maintenance assessments, progress reporting, and R&M history access **to at least 97%** (S/M), **through the mobile self-service features of VRAMS-Mobile** (A/R), **within the first year of operation** (T).
4. **Increase** customer loyalty (repeat customers) **to 90% of the year-over-year customer base** (S/M), **by improving appointment convenience via VRAMS-Mobile** (A/R), **within the first year of operation** (T).
5. **Deliver** a publicly-tested VRAMS-Mobile prototype for iPhone, iPad, and Galaxy Android (S), **approved by the project's technical committee** (M/A), **within 60 days (week 12) of project launch** (T/R).
