# Worked Example: VRAMS-Mobile Project Charter

*(RM Vehicle Repair & Maintenance Shop)*

**Business need:** 85% of customers prefer booking via mobile, but the current VRAMS system isn't mobile-friendly → most bookings still go through costly customer service reps (9% of annual operating cost).

**Business objectives:**
- Cut annual appointment servicing cost to **5%** of annual operating cost (year 1)
- Generate **≥ $400,000/year** in mobile ad revenue
- Raise customer satisfaction to **≥ 97%**
- Raise customer loyalty (repeat customers) to **90%**

**Success criteria:** Prototype tested in 60 days (week 12); fully functional app deployed by week 24.

**Constraints:** Must use existing dev/execution servers; must follow the company's Rational Unified Process (RUP) methodology.

**Budget:** $210,000 + 20% contingency ($42,000) = **$252,000 total**

**Milestones:** Project start (wk 1) → Planning (wk 3) → Prototype (wk 12) → Dev & testing (wk 21) → Deployment (wk 24)
