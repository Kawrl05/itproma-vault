# Project Charter — 5 Key Inputs (PMBOK)

1. **Statement of Work (SOW)** – narrative: business need, product description, strategic alignment
2. **Business Case** – project's value (financial: revenue/cost; non-financial: loyalty, innovation)
3. **Agreement** – for contract-based projects (contract SOW + terms/conditions)
4. **Enterprise Environmental Factors (EEFs)** – internal (org structure, HR skills, PMO policies) & external (competition, regulations, market conditions) factors
5. **Organizational Process Assets (OPAs)** – policies, templates, prior-project docs, lessons learned
