# Worked Example: Construction Project Charter (Luxury Apartments at York)

**Vision:** Complete on time, within budget, to meet local housing needs.

**Business case:** District is growing; area lacks luxury residential housing.

**Timeline:** Start Q4, complete in 12–16 months.

**Budget:** $15 million.

**Key constraint:** ≤ 18 months; budget includes a contingency fund and must not be exceeded.

**Key risks:** Material cost spikes, resource unavailability, equipment damage/theft, natural disasters.
