# Project Charter

A document issued by the **project initiator/sponsor** that formally **authorizes the project's existence** and gives the PM authority to apply organizational resources.

Written during the **Initiation** phase, before detailed planning. Ties the project to the organization's strategy.

## Why It Matters (3 main uses)
1. **Authorizes the project** – sells it to stakeholders (like an elevator pitch)
2. **Primary sales document** – summary stakeholders can share/reference
3. **Stays with the project** throughout the lifecycle as a high-level roadmap

## Without a Charter, Projects Risk:
- Unclear need / unstable direction
- Ambiguous objectives & scope
- Ambiguous solution
- Missed key stakeholders
