# Resource Management — Processes

1. **Plan Resource Management** – identify roles, responsibilities, skills, reporting structure → produces the **HR management plan**
   - Org charts can be Hierarchical, Matrix, or Text
2. **Estimate Activity Resources** – estimate team + physical/material resources & quantities/durations needed
3. **Acquire Resources** – fill skill gaps; negotiate with other PMs/functional managers; consider pre-assignment
4. **Develop Team** – improve skills & team environment (interpersonal skills, team-building, ground rules, co-location, war room, rewards, performance assessments) → follows [[Tuckmans Ladder]]
5. **Manage Team** – track performance, give feedback, resolve issues (issue logs, performance appraisals) → draws on [[Powers of the Project Manager]] and [[Conflict Management Styles]]
6. **Control Resources** – ensure physical resources are available as planned; monitor planned vs. actual use

## HR Management Plan Contents
- Roles & responsibilities (roles, authority, responsibilities, competency)
- Project organization charts
- Staffing management plan (resource calendar, staff release plan, training, rewards, compliance, safety)

## Key Terms
- **Halo Effect** – rating someone high/low on everything because of one strong impression
- **Virtual Teams** – don't meet face-to-face; cheaper but risk miscommunication & isolation
- **Pre-assignment** – people assigned before the project starts (e.g., named in the proposal)
